# Ultra Vision roadmap

The roadmap is organized around product outcomes, not feature volume.

## Now — make the first mix undeniable

- Zero-setup demo tracks and a guided first-run path.
- Intentional gesture clutch and no-jump takeover for every control.
- Deterministic camera, gesture, audio, and permission tests.
- Current screenshots, release notes, contributor docs, and a tagged release.
- Measured startup time, frame rate, control latency, and 30-minute stability.

## Next — make it musically intelligent

- Waveforms, beat positions, downbeats, and confidence-aware BPM analysis.
- Phase-aware sync against one shared transport clock.
- Local calibration profiles and dominant-hand selection.
- Keyboard performance shortcuts and a compact mobile master strip.
- Privacy-safe local recording of short performance clips.

## Later — expand the vision instrument

- Optional open-vocabulary grounding behind a separate adapter.
- Gesture mapping presets and community-authored control packs.
- Additional local effects with a measured audio-performance budget.
- A benchmark gallery for devices, browsers, lighting, and camera quality.

## Non-goals

- Uploading camera or music by default.
- Claiming beat alignment from BPM matching alone.
- Presenting pixel heuristics as neural-model output.
- Bundling copyrighted demo music.
- Making the core DJ experience depend on a large hosted model.
